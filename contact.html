Customer Support FAQ

Quick answers to common order, shipping, and alteration questions

What happens if my custom blouse needs fitting adjustments?
Every custom blouse is crafted with 2-inch interior seam margins. We also provide free pickup and re-stitching within 10 days of delivery.
Do you ship internationally outside India?
Yes! We ship worldwide via DHL Express. International orders usually arrive within 7 to 10 business days.
Can I cancel or alter a custom order after placing it?
You can modify measurements or design details within 24 hours of order placement by contacting our WhatsApp support team.
How do I track my order status?
Once dispatched, you will receive an SMS and email notification with an active tracking link.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit/Debit Cards, Net Banking, and Cash on Delivery (COD for standard items).
