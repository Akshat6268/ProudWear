The ProudeWear Story

Preserving Heritage, Redefining Fit

Born out of a passion for authentic Indian handloom textiles and seamless bespoke tailoring, ProudeWear bridges centuries of artisan legacy with modern minimalistic silhouettes.

Our Story & Mission

For generations, the saree blouse was considered an afterthought — ill-fitting ready-mades or nerve-wracking visits to neighborhood tailors with endless alteration delays. At ProudeWear, we believe every saree deserves a companion blouse crafted with mathematical precision and artistic perfection.

We source pure silk, handloom brocades, and lightweight organza directly from master weaving clusters in Kanchipuram, Varanasi, Chanderi, and Bengal. Every garment is cut individually and hand-embroidered by artisan craftspeople.

Whether it is a high-profile wedding reception or a lightweight office drape, our studio delivers couture precision right to your doorstep.

Artisan Craftsmanship

Inside our studio: From raw silk skeins to finished couture

1. Ethical Handloom Sourcing

We hand-select pure silk yarns, zari cords, and organza directly from weaver clusters in Banaras and Kanchipuram.

2. Master Tailoring & Zardozi

Our master artisans hand-embroider intricate thread, stone, and maggam motifs before precision cutting.

3. Double Quality Inspection

Every seam, hook closure, and padded cup undergoes strict tensile and measurement verification before dispatch.
