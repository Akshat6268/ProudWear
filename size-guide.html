7-Point Measuring Callout Guide

Follow these exact measurement instructions using a flexible measuring tape

1
Bust: Measure around the fullest part of your bust while wearing your regular bra.
2
Under Bust: Measure directly below the bust line where the blouse rib band sits.
3
Waist: Measure around your natural waistline just above the navel.
4
Shoulder: Measure across the back from shoulder tip to shoulder tip.
5
Armhole: Wrap the tape around the arm socket where the sleeve attaches.
6
Sleeve Length: Measure from the shoulder tip down to desired sleeve hem.
7
Blouse Length: Measure from highest shoulder point down to desired lower waist hem.
